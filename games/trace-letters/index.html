🤔 How to play

  1. Put your finger (or mouse) on the green dot.
  2. Trace slowly along the grey path — your line paints in as you go!
  3. Follow the little arrow, and do the strokes in number order.
  4. Not sure how? Tap 👀 Show me to watch first.
  5. Traced it? Tap Again with less help — the letter fades away until you can write it all by yourself!

This activity is traced by touch or mouse. Grown-ups: tracing with one finger, nice and slow, is great pencil practice.
