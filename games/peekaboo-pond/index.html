🤔 How to play

  1. Press the big ▶ button.
  2. Watch! A critter hops into a pink flower, and the flower closes.
  3. The flowers slide around. Keep your eyes on the right one.
  4. Tap the flower where the critter is hiding. Peekaboo!

A wrong flower just holds a surprise, a goldfish or a ladybug, so there is no failing. The game starts with two flowers that stay still, and only adds flowers and moves as your child finds the critter first time. Five finds bring a little party. On a keyboard, the arrow keys choose a flower and Enter opens it. Nothing is saved.

What it practises

Following a moving object with the eyes, remembering where something is when it cannot be seen, and patience: the skills behind every good game of peekaboo, with Clover, Pip, Plum and Mango taking turns to hide.
