🤔 How to play

  1. Press the big ▶ button.
  2. Balloons float up into the sky, and a voice asks for a colour: "Pop the red balloon!" The colour also shows at the top.
  3. Tap the balloon in that colour. Pop!
  4. A wrong balloon just wiggles and says its own colour. After two tries, the right one bounces to help.

It starts with just red and blue, and adds yellow, green, orange and purple as your child gets them right first time. Five pops bring a little party. The 🔊 button says the colour again. On a keyboard, the arrow keys choose a balloon and Enter pops it. Nothing is saved.

The voice is your device's own built-in voice, so nothing is sent anywhere. If the device has none, the colour at the top shows what to find.

What it practises

Hearing a colour's name and finding it: the step from matching colours to knowing their names, with Clover, Pip, Plum and Mango cheering every pop.
