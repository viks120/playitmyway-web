🤔 How to play

  • A card appears — look at it, then tap the bin where it belongs.
  • Every card tells you why, whether you were right or not. Reading the reason is the game!
  • Tricky cards pop back a few cards later for a second try — get them then for half the stars.
  • Keyboard: press 1 for the left bin, 2 for the right bin, and Enter for the next card.
  • Nothing is saved — your best score lasts only for this visit.
