🤔 How to play

  1. Press Start to play Numbers, or tap Letters or Free Pop to start a new game in that mode straight away. Balloons with numbers or letters on them bob in the sky, and the box above says which one to pop next.
  2. In Numbers, pop 1, then 2, then 3, all the way to 10. In Letters, pop A, then B, then C, up to F. Each right pop scores 10 points.
  3. You have 30 seconds. Finish the run and you go up a level with 15 more seconds on the clock. Each new level makes the run longer (up to 14, then 16, then 18 and so on in Numbers; up to J, then L and so on in Letters; never past 26 or Z) and puts one more balloon in the sky, up to 12.
  4. When the time runs out, you see your score, your level and one to three stars. Free Pop has no order and no levels: pop any balloon for 10 points before the clock runs out.

Controls

  • Touch or mouse: tap or click a balloon to pop it.
  • Keyboard: every balloon is a button. Tab moves between them and Enter or Space pops the one you are on. Esc closes the end-of-game box.

A wrong balloon does not pop. It costs 5 points (the score never drops below 0) and the game says what you hit and what it wants, like "That was 7 — we're looking for 3!". The best score lasts only this visit; nothing is saved.

What it practises

Finding the next number or letter in order among a jumble of others, against the clock. You have to recognise each written numeral or letter by sight, not just say the sequence.
