What's Different?

Tap what's different!

🤔 How to play

  1. Look at the two pictures. They are nearly the same.
  2. Tap anything that is different, in either picture. A ring shows every one you find.
  3. Find them all and a new picture comes. It gets a little trickier only as your child gets them.

If nothing is found for a while, one difference glows and the voice says "Look here!". Wrong taps never matter. With a keyboard, Tab into a picture, move with the arrow keys and press Enter. The voice is your device's own; nothing is sent anywhere, and nothing is saved.

What it practises

Looking carefully and comparing: noticing that something is missing, a different colour, or bigger, and holding one picture in mind while looking at the other.
