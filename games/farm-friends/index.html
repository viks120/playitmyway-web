🤔 How to play

  1. Press the big ▶ button.
  2. Animals pop up in the farmyard, and a voice asks: "Where's the cow?" A picture shows at the top, and you hear the cow moo.
  3. Tap the cow! It hops for joy and hearts float up.
  4. Tap a different animal and it says who it is: "That's the pig. Oink!" After two tries, the right one bounces to help.

Eight animals join in turn: cow and duck first, then pig, sheep, dog, cat, horse and chicken, as your child finds them first time. Five finds bring a little party. The 🔊 button says it again. On a keyboard, the arrow keys choose an animal and Enter picks it. Nothing is saved.

The voice is your device's own built-in voice, so nothing is sent anywhere. The animal sounds are made right in the browser.

What it practises

Animal names and the sounds they make, listening, and pointing to the one that was asked for: early words, with every wrong tap turning into a new word.
