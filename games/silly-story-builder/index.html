🤔 How to play

  • Every blank in the story shows a colour and a label — that tells you which kind of word it needs.
  • The word tray mixes all four kinds together. Find a word of the right kind and tap it.
  • Picked the wrong kind? No problem — you get a friendly hint, and then the right kind of words start to glow.
  • When every blank is filled, read your story out loud. Then tap 🎲 Change one word and watch one word flip the whole story.
  • Tap 🖨️ Print my story to keep your story on paper — nothing is saved here, so every visit starts fresh.
