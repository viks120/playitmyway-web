⌨️ Click the clock, then: arrows = 1 minute · Shift+arrows = 5 · Page Up/Down = 1 hour · Enter = check

🤔 How to play

  1. The short hand tells the hour. The long hand tells the minutes.
  2. Drag the long hand all the way around and watch closely — the short hand creeps to the next number. That's how real clocks work!
  3. Set the clock: make the clock say the time a customer asks for, then press Check.
  4. Read the clock: look at the hands and pick the right time.
  5. Pick your level, from O'clock up to Any minute. Nothing is saved — your score resets when you leave.
