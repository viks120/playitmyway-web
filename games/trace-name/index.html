🤔 How to play

  1. A grown-up types a name or word and taps Make it!
  2. Trace each letter — put your finger on the green dot and follow the path.
  3. Finish a letter and the next one appears. Finish them all to see your whole word! 🎉
  4. Not sure how? Tap 👀 Show me to watch first.
  5. Wrote it? Tap Again with less help — the letters fade away until you can write your name all by yourself!

Nothing typed here is saved or sent anywhere — it disappears when you leave the page. Tracing their own name is one of the most motivating ways for a child to start writing.
