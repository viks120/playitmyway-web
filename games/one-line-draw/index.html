🤔 How to play

  1. Tap any dot to start. On tricky shapes, where you start matters!
  2. Drag (or tap dot after dot) to draw along the lines.
  3. Cover every line exactly once to win. You can't draw the same line twice!
  4. Stuck? Use Undo, Restart, or a Hint 💡
  5. Ready for a real challenge? Turn on 🖊️ No lifting — one single stroke, and lifting your finger starts the shape over!
