🤔 How to play

  1. Pick your critter and a level: easy ones have one pebble, tricky ones have three.
  2. Hop round the lily pads. Hop into a pebble to push it one pad along.
  3. Push every pebble onto a pink flower. The flower blooms round it!
  4. Pebbles can only be pushed, never pulled, so think before you push. A pebble stuck in a corner can't come out: tap ↩️ Undo.

To hop, use the arrow buttons, swipe, tap a lily pad next to your critter, or press ← ↑ → ↓. Z undoes, R restarts and H or 💡 shows a hint. Every level has been checked by computer, so every one can be finished. Nothing is saved: ticks for finished levels last only this visit.

What it practises

Planning ahead and spotting dead ends: thinking "if I push it there, can I still get behind it?" before acting, and using undo to learn from a wrong turn.
