Spot the Difference

Find all 5 differences. Tap them in either picture.

Round 1 ★ 0

🤔 How to play

  1. The two pictures look the same, but five or more things are different: something missing, a different colour or shade, bigger or smaller, flipped, moved, tilted, or one more apple than before.
  2. Tap each difference in either picture. A ring marks it in both.
  3. Find them all for stars: three with no hint, two with one, one with more. Stuck? 💡 Hint shows one; "Show me the rest" gives up the round.

There is no clock and wrong taps cost nothing. Each round has a few more, and smaller, differences than the last. With a keyboard, Tab into a picture, move with the arrow keys and press Enter. Nothing is saved: the stars last only this visit.

What it practises

Careful, systematic looking and visual memory: comparing two scenes part by part, noticing small changes of colour, size, position and number, and keeping track of what has been checked.
