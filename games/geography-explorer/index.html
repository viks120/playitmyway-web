🤔 How to play

  1. Pick a challenge type. Countries names a country and shows its flag: pick the continent it is on. Capitals asks for a country's capital city. Flags shows a flag and asks which country it belongs to.
  2. In Capitals and Flags there are four answers, and the three wrong ones come from the same continent as the right one, so knowing the region is not enough.
  3. A right answer on the first try scores 50 points; on the second try it scores half. In Countries mode, once the answer is shown (right, or after two misses), a red pin drops on the map to show where the country is.
  4. Press Next for a new question. The country is picked at random from 30, so one can come up again. Every 5 right answers takes you up a level. The questions do not get harder.

Controls

  • Touch or mouse: tap an answer button. In Countries mode you can also tap a continent on the world map. Get Hint gives one clue per question; a right answer after it is worth 40 points instead of 50, and half that on a second try. New Challenge skips to a new random question.
  • Keyboard: Tab moves between the answers and the continents on the map, and Enter or Space picks one. Esc closes the level-up box.

A wrong first answer greys out and a clue about the country appears, such as "Home to the Amazon rainforest and Carnival". A second wrong answer shows the right one with that fact. Nothing is saved: the score starts again at 0 next visit.

What it practises

Which continent 30 countries are on, their capital cities and their flags, telling apart neighbours from the same continent.
