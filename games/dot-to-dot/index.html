🤔 How to play

  1. Find the dot with the number 1 and tap it.
  2. Then tap 2, then 3 — count out loud as you go!
  3. The glowing dot is always the next one. Tapping a wrong dot is fine, nothing breaks.
  4. Join every dot to finish the picture and see what it is 🎉
