🤔 How to play

  • Look at the picture, then tap the letters to fill the three sound boxes — first sound, middle sound, last sound.
  • Tap ▶ Blend and watch the word light up letter by letter — slowly, then fast. Say each sound as it glows!
  • Tap ✔ Check when the word looks right. A wrong try gets a friendly sound hint — never a penalty.
  • In a Word Ladder, each new word changes just one sound of the last word. Fix the empty box!
  • Keyboard: type a letter to use its tile, Backspace to take one back, Enter to check.
  • Nothing is saved — your best streak lasts only for this visit.
